Privacy Policy — KIIP Level 1

Effective: 2026-11-02 · Applies to the iOS app “KIIP Level 1” (bundle id com.dash2zero.kiip1)

Summary

The app works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no server of its own, and it does not send your study data anywhere. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your settings: test date, class progress, language, sound/vibration preferences, and whether you turned on the Korean overlay.
  • Your study records: quiz answers, mock-test scores, review schedule and speaking-practice checklists.
  • Voice recordings you make in Speaking practice. They are saved only on your phone, are never uploaded, and are deleted automatically after 30 days or when you delete the app or use “Delete all my data” in Settings.

This data is included in your normal iOS backups (iCloud or computer backups) according to your own iOS settings. We cannot access those backups.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only when you tap Record in Speaking practice. You can refuse; the app then offers listen-and-repeat instead.

Crash reports

If you agree to it in Settings, the app may send anonymous crash reports (device model, iOS version, app version, stack trace) to our crash-reporting provider so we can fix bugs. These reports never include your recordings, answers or settings. This is off by default.

Optional AI practice partner (future update)

A future version may offer an optional AI speaking partner. If released, it will be off by default and ask for your separate consent. It would send only the text transcript of your answer (never audio) to our processing partner, Anthropic PBC (United States), to generate feedback. We will update this policy before that feature is enabled.

Children

The app is intended for adult learners enrolled in the Korea Immigration and Integration Program. It is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete everything with Settings → “Delete all my data”, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn crash reports on or off at any time in Settings.
