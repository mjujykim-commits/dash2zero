iOS app “KIIP Level 1: Stage Test Prep” (bundle id com.dash2zero.kiip1)

About the app

KIIP Level 1 helps you prepare for the Level 1 stage test of the Korea Immigration and Integration Program: the 18 textbook units (vocabulary, grammar, culture and life-information cards), practice by question type, full-length mock tests in the real format (20 questions, 30 minutes, answer sheet) and the five speaking tasks with recording. It works offline and has no account.

Frequently asked questions

Are these the real test questions?

No. Every question, passage and model answer is original practice material written for this app in the official format. The app is not affiliated with the Ministry of Justice of Korea or the institutions that run the program. Always confirm the current test rules with your institution.

What does the predicted score mean?

It is an estimate based on your practice results, shown against the 60-point pass line. It is not a prediction of your real test score.

Why does the app ask for the microphone?

Only to record your answers in Speaking practice so you can compare them with the model audio. Recordings stay on your phone. If you refuse the permission, you can still listen and repeat.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and choose “Delete all my data”, or delete the app. Nothing is stored outside your phone.

Can I show Korean next to the English?

Yes. Turn on the Korean overlay in Settings. It is meant for studying with a spouse, friend or teacher who reads Korean.

I found a mistake in a question.

Email the unit or mock-test number and the question text. Corrections ship in the next content update.
